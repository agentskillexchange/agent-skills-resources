# Compact Evaluator Outcome Example

This illustrative example shows how to turn a filled evaluation worksheet into
a short recommendation. It is a decision pattern, not an endorsement of a
specific skill, framework, or catalog entry.

## Evaluation Snapshot

| Worksheet evidence | What the evaluator recorded |
|---|---|
| Target workflow | Draft a cited comparison of public product documentation for an editor to review. |
| Scope and access | Public, approved domains only; no repository, customer-data, secret, or production access. |
| Verification | Save the source list, citation coverage check, weak-claim notes, and the editor's disposition. |
| Main risk | A fluent draft could make an unsupported or stale claim look credible. |
| Missing evidence | The target team's authoritative-source rules and an editor review of one private draft. |

## Final Recommendation

**Decision: Revisit later — do not start a pilot yet.**

The workflow is narrow and its proposed access boundary is appropriate for a
future pilot. However, the team has not yet supplied the source-quality rules
or shown that an editor can review and disposition the claim trail. Starting a
pilot before those two checks would turn an evidence gap into an implicit
approval.

## What To Record In The Worksheet

| Field | Filled outcome |
|---|---|
| Decision reason | Useful workflow and bounded public-data scope, but source rules and editor review evidence are incomplete. |
| Blocking reason or missing evidence | Approved source hierarchy and one private draft with citation coverage, weak-claim notes, and editor disposition. |
| Owner for the follow-up | Editorial owner for the intended audience and topic. |
| Evidence needed before another review | Approved source hierarchy plus the saved review artifacts from one private draft. |
| Date or trigger to revisit | Reopen when the editorial owner has reviewed the draft artifacts against the agreed source rules. |

## Boundary For The Next Decision

After the missing evidence is available, reopen the same worksheet. Choose
`Pilot` only if the new artifacts show a reviewable claim trail and the scope
remains public, bounded, and approval-gated. If the workflow now needs private
material, production access, or broader writes before that evidence exists,
stop and use the [Risk Review](../../templates/risk-review.md) instead.

For a fuller evidence shape, compare the
[Content Research Evaluation](content-research-evaluation.md); keep its
evidence categories, but write a recommendation that is specific to your own
workflow.
