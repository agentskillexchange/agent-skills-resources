# Content Research Evaluation

This is an illustrative completed worksheet, not a certification claim.

## Skill

- Skill slug: `draft-cited-research-reports-with-storm`
- Workflow: cited research draft with editorial review
- Source of example: representative mapping in `data/ase-skill-mapping.json`
- Decision: revisit after source-quality and claim-review evidence is complete

## Workflow Fit

The skill fits research, content, documentation, and strategy teams that want an
agent to gather public sources, draft cited material, and keep unsupported
claims visible before an editor decides whether anything is publishable.

## Permissions Reviewed

- Repo access: not required for the first evaluation.
- Data access: public sources only.
- Network access: limited to approved research domains and public search.
- Production access: not approved for the first evaluation.
- Human approval: required from an editorial owner before publication.

## Verification Evidence Example

```text
Pilot topic: public product documentation comparison
Allowed sources: official docs, public changelogs, vendor blogs, public papers
Excluded sources: private customer notes, internal roadmaps, anonymous forums
Expected evidence: source list, citation coverage, weak-claim notes,
editorial comments, publish or reject decision
```

## Risk Review Summary

- Main risk: polished copy hides weak sourcing, outdated facts, or unsupported
  claims.
- Mitigation: require a source list before drafting, mark every weak claim, and
  keep the draft private until an editor records the publish or reject decision.
- Open question: which sources count as authoritative for the target audience
  and topic.

## Why A Team Might Pilot It

- The workflow can start with public sources and no production credentials.
- Editors can review the claim trail before anything is shared externally.
- The output has a clear decision point: publish, revise, or reject.

## Why A Team Might Reject It

- The final claims cannot be traced back to the source list.
- Citation coverage is missing for important claims.
- The workflow needs confidential or regulated material before source-quality
  rules are agreed.
